
## Official statistics: the embargo

- **Pre-release figures are embargoed** until their publication date. Do not open, print or summarize files in the output folders before then, unless the organization's AI-use policy allows the AI service in use to see pre-release figures. Never paste them into issues, pull requests or messages.
- **Output folders are never committed.** The `.gitignore` and `tools/check.R` refuse them. A release record (`releases/<vintage>/`) is committed only after publication, in the release pull request.
- **Deposits and releases are made by a person.** Never write to Teams, Z:, SDMX or data.unicef.org, and never prepare a release record unless asked.
